FAQ

How long does a website take?

A landing page typically ships in about two weeks; full websites in four to six. The timeline is agreed in writing with the scope.

Who owns the website?

You do, 100%. Code, design files, content, accounts, and domain are registered to you and handed over at launch.

What happens after launch?

Every build includes a post-launch support period, and optional care plans are available if you want us to keep maintaining and improving the site.
